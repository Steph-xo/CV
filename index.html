💡 Reflection

Human migration is one of the most defining forces in history. The “Great Migration” of Homo sapiens not only explains how humans populated the globe but also highlights how environment, adaptation, and survival shaped identity, culture, and society. Understanding these early migrations helps us appreciate the roots of human diversity and the connections that unite us across continents.
