Module 1 Project: Interactive Map of Human Migrations

Waves of Homo sapiens Migration

🎯 Objective

Students will demonstrate a complex understanding of the phenomenon of human migration by exploring the causes, impacts, and importance of human migration across the ages.

📌 Method

Group work (3–4 students per team). The project will use portfolio format and include research notes, storyboard, bibliography, and an interactive map.

📚 Resources

  • StoryMap JS platform
  • Activity 5.1 Notes
  • Project Rubric (see attached)
  • Research bibliography (Nature, Smithsonian, National Geographic, etc.)

🗺️ Interactive Map

This map shows the “Great Migration” of Homo sapiens: from Africa into the Middle East (~210,000 years ago), later expanding into Europe and Asia.

📝 Instructions

  1. Research your assigned migration and draft a storyboard.
  2. Create an interactive StoryMap with at least 10 slides.
  3. Include locations, routes, images, objects, and key people.
  4. Each slide must have a title, image, and explanatory text.
  5. Add proper citations and bibliography in APA format.

💡 Reflection

How do human migrations shape identity, culture, and the development of civilizations? Connect your migration map with themes from Module 1, such as resources, environment, war, trade, religion, and politics.
